## Game description

Donkey Kong is an arcade game released in 1981 where you control the Jumpman, your objective is to reach the top of a building to rescue Pauline, who has been kidnapped by Donkey Kong, who will be throwing barrels at you. But, in this version of our game, you control Subaru and you must climb to the top of a tower to reach till Regulus, who will be the one throwing the barrels.


## Gameplay Mechanics
Your main objective is to climb to the top of the tower using the stairs and avoid the obstacles that will be thrown to kill you.

| Button|Action|
|-------|------|
|D|Move to the right|
|A|Move to the left|
|W|Jump|
|W(close to stairs)|Climb|

| Item |Function|
|-------|------|
|Nuke|Destroy all barrels on screen and stuns Remulus|
|Beatrice|Shoots at mouse position|
