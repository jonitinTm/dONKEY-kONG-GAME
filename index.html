DONKEY KONG
SUBARU'S CLIMB

A 2D arcade platformer built in C++ & Raylib — a tribute to the original Donkey Kong (1981).

The Game

Donkey Kong is the 1981 arcade classic where you control Jumpman, climbing a construction site to rescue Pauline from a barrel-throwing ape. In our version, you play as Subaru, climbing to the top of a tower to reach Regulus — the one hurling the barrels down at you.

Dodge barrels, scale ladders, grab power-ups and fight your way up across 5 levels. The project also ships with a full in-game level editor, GPU-based 2D lighting, and a keyframe cinematic system.

  • Win condition: Climb the tower and reach Regulus at the top.
  • Lose condition: Lose all 3 lives to the barrels and hazards.
  • Items: Nuke (clears barrels & stuns Regulus) & Beatrice (shoots at the cursor).
  • Power-ups: Dash, Shield, Whip, Extra Life, Larper and more.
  • Enemy: Regulus, with a full jump/stun AI state machine.
